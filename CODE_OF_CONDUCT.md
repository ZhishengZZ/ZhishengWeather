# 行为准则 // CODE OF CONDUCT

参照 [Contributor Covenant 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/) 制定，适用于本仓库全部社区空间（Issue / PR / Discussions）。

*Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/); it applies to every community space of this repository (issues / PRs / Discussions).*

## 01// 我们的承诺 OUR PLEDGE

营造一个开放、友好、不骚扰的参与环境。不因身份、经验、背景设门槛——终端面前，人人平等。

*We pledge an open, welcoming, harassment-free environment. No barriers of identity, experience or background — at the terminal, everyone is equal.*

## 02// 期望行为 EXPECTED

- 尊重不同观点，善意解读别人的代码和意见
  *Respect differing views; read other people's code and comments charitably.*
- 给得出，也接得住建设性反馈
  *Give constructive feedback gracefully, and take it the same way.*
- 为自己的错误负责，及时修正
  *Own your mistakes and fix them promptly.*
- 多想着项目整体，别只盯着自己
  *Think of the whole project, not just your corner of it.*

## 03// 不可接受 UNACCEPTABLE

- 侮辱、歧视、骚扰性言论
  *Insulting, discriminatory or harassing language.*
- 公开他人隐私（包括凭据、个人信息）
  *Publishing others' privacy — credentials and personal data included.*
- 垃圾广告、商业推广、与项目无关的煽动内容
  *Spam, commercial promotion, or agitation unrelated to the project.*
- 任何形式的威胁或恶意破坏
  *Threats or sabotage in any form.*

## 04// 报告 REPORTING

- 私信维护者，或走仓库的 Security 通道报告
  *Message the maintainer privately, or report through the repository's Security channel.*
- 报告保密处理；核实后删除违规内容、限制违规账号互动
  *Reports are handled confidentially; verified abuse gets removed and the offending account restricted.*
- 善意报告者不会因为报告而受到任何形式的报复
  *Good-faith reporters face no retaliation of any kind.*

## 05// 执行 SCOPE & ENFORCEMENT

维护者对行为准则拥有解释与执行权；重大争议以 Contributor Covenant 2.0 原文为准。

*The maintainer holds interpretation and enforcement authority; serious disputes defer to the Contributor Covenant 2.0 original text.*
